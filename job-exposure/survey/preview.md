# AI and Nigerian remote work: which tasks can AI do?

I'm studying which parts of common Nigerian remote and freelance jobs AI tools can do today. You'll rate the tasks in your own job. It takes about 5 minutes.

There are no right answers. Your experience is the point, and it's fine if you disagree with what AI companies say.

Your answers are anonymous: no name, phone number or email is collected. Results are published only as totals and short anonymous quotes. This is an independent study by Olaoluwa Oluwasanya (olaoluwa.work).
