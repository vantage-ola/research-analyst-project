# Policy rubric (v1)

Score each document on 10 criteria, 0 to 2. Score only from what the document itself says. Don't use outside knowledge of what happened after it was published.

Every score needs evidence: the PDF page number and an exact quote of 15 words or fewer, copied word for word from that page. If the document has nothing on a criterion, the score is 0 with no page and no quote, and the reason says what you looked for.

When a document says something in several places, cite the strongest single passage. If a criterion sits between two levels, pick the lower one and say why.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Binding force | The document presents itself as a strategy, vision or framework, with no stated approval. | The document states it was approved or adopted by cabinet, parliament or an equivalent body, but it is not law. | The document is, or states it creates, a law or regulation with legal force. |
| 2 | Responsible body | No body is named as responsible for carrying the document out. | A body is named, but its duties are unclear or responsibility is spread loosely across many bodies. | A named body has stated duties for carrying out the document. |
| 3 | Funding | Money or budget is not mentioned. | Funding is mentioned, but with no amount or no source. | An amount and a source of funding are both stated. |
| 4 | Measurable targets | There are no numerical targets. | There are numbers without deadlines, or deadlines without numbers. | There are numerical targets with deadlines. |
| 5 | Jobs and workforce | Jobs and the workforce are not covered. | Skills or training goals only. | Skills goals plus plans for workers whose jobs AI affects (transition, retraining for displaced workers, social protection). |
| 6 | Risk and safety | AI risks are not covered. | General ethics principles or a general mention of risks. | Specific risk measures: risk assessments, high-risk categories, testing or audits, or a named safety mechanism. |
| 7 | Data protection link | Data protection and privacy are not mentioned. | Privacy or data protection is mentioned in general. | A named data protection law or data protection regulator is linked to the document. |
| 8 | Enforcement | Nothing on enforcement. | It says rules or regulation will be developed later. | Penalties, sanctions or an enforcement process are stated. |
| 9 | Local language and context | Local languages and context are not covered. | Local languages or local context are mentioned as a goal. | Concrete commitments for local languages: datasets, models, or funding named for them. |
| 10 | Monitoring and evaluation | No monitoring or review. | Review or monitoring is promised with no method. | Named indicators, a named reviewer, and a schedule. |

## Vague commitments

List 5 to 10 commitments that are hard to hold anyone to. For each, give the page, an exact quote of 15 words or fewer, and which of these are missing: owner, number, date, money.

## AI safety check

Answer yes or no for each of these. For yes, give the page and an exact quote of 15 words or fewer.

- **frontier_risk:** does it mention risks from the most advanced or general-purpose AI models (frontier models, foundation models, AGI, or similar)?
- **model_evaluation:** does it mention evaluating, testing, auditing or red-teaming AI models or systems before or after deployment?
- **incident_reporting:** does it mention reporting, recording or responding to AI incidents or harms?
